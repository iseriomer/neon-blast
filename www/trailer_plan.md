# Neon Blast - Game Trailer Plan & Template

## 🎥 Duration
**Recommended Duration:** **30 - 45 seconds**.
*   This is the ideal length for mobile and web game trailers. Attention spans are short; keep it punchy, fast, and impactful.
*   The first **3 seconds** are critical. You must show the most intense action right here.

---

## 🎬 Editing Structure (Post-Production Template)

Use this template as a timeline for your video editing. Cutting to the beat of the music is crucial.

### Part 1: The Hook | 00:00 - 00:05
*   **Goal:** Make the viewer think, "This looks chaotic and fun!"
*   **Visuals:** A 2-3 second clip from the late game where the screen is filled with bullets and effects. Maybe the moment "The Omega" boss's shield breaks or the "Black Hole" skill swallows enemies.
*   **Text Overlay:** 
    *   **READY FOR CHAOS?**

### Part 2: Progression | 00:05 - 00:15
*   **Goal:** Showcase the Power Fantasy (growth).
*   **Visuals:** 
    1.  Quick shot of the Level 1 ship firing slowly (1 sec).
    2.  Immediately cut to the "Level Up" screen and picking a cool "Perk" (e.g., Laser Beam or Split Shot) (1 sec).
    3.  Cut to the ship's weapons changing and firing more powerfully (2 sec).
    4.  Another Level Up, showing the screen filling up with projectiles (2 sec).
*   **Text Overlay:**
    *   **UPGRADE. EVOLVE. DOMINATE.**

### Part 3: Action & Bosses (Climax) | 00:15 - 00:25
*   **Goal:** Show variety and difficulty.
*   **Visuals:** Rapid cuts of boss fights.
    *   **Boss 1 (The Swarm):** Surrounded by drones.
    *   **Boss 3 (The Omega):** Shield mechanics and lasers.
    *   **Boss 5 (Chronos):** Time-bending effects or chaotic bullet patterns.
    *   Use "God Mode" here to show off the coolest "Perk" combinations (Orbitals + Chain Lightning + Homing).
*   **Text Overlay:**
    *   **FIGHT EPIC BOSSES**

### Part 4: Call to Action (Outro) | 00:25 - 00:30
*   **Goal:** Invite the player to play.
*   **Visuals:** The game logo (Neon Blast) on a black background or over blurred gameplay footage.
*   **Text Overlay:**
    *   **PLAY NOW!**
*   **Subtitle:** Website link or platform logos.

## 📝 Copywriting Suggestions

Short and punchy text you can use in the video:

**Option 1 (Action Focused):**
1. SURVIVE THE CHAOS
2. CHOOSE YOUR WEAPON
3. DESTROY THEM ALL

**Option 2 (Progression Focused):**
1. START SMALL
2. GROW GIANT
3. CONQUER THE UNIVERSE

**Option 3 (Feature Focused - Neon Theme):**
1. BRIGHT LIGHTS
2. ENDLESS ACTION
3. NEON BLAST

## 💡 Tips

1.  **Music:** Choose the most rhythmic Synthwave/Cyberpunk track you have. Try to change scenes on the beat.
2.  **Zoom:** If recording in a browser, record only the `canvas` area or the game area including the `ui-layer`. Do not show the browser address bar.
3.  **Mouse Cursor:** Ensure the mouse cursor is hidden or doesn't ruin the aesthetic during recording.
4.  **Admin Panel:** Use the `admin.js` features (God Mode, Level Up, Spawn Boss) to easily create and record those "overpowered" moments that are normally hard to reach.
